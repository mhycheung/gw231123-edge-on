Density of distinguishable NRSur7dq4 waveforms per unit prior probability, $s$, against the inclination at merger, for prior draws in the GW231123 mass window (set W). The plot answers whether edge-on orientations at merger have more distinguishable waveforms per unit prior probability than other orientations, and whether that depends on in-plane spin (precession).

$s=\sqrt{\det g^\theta}/\pi_\theta(\theta)$, where $g^\theta$ is the mismatch metric of the unit-normalised two-detector (H1, L1) signal over $\theta=(M,q,\vec\chi_1,\vec\chi_2,\iota_Q(0))$ with $\phi_{\rm ref}$, $\psi$ and $t_c$ profiled out, and $\pi_\theta$ is the prior density of the parameter estimation (PE) on $\theta$. The mean of $s$ over prior draws in a region is the metric volume of the region divided by its prior probability. $\iota_Q(0)$ is the angle between the line of sight and the axis of the surrogate's coprecessing frame at $t=0$, the peak of the total mode amplitude. Horizontal axis: $|\cos\iota_Q(0)|$ (0 is edge-on, 1 is face-on or face-off); the prior is uniform in it. Vertical axis: $\log_{10}s$, where $s$ has the units of $\theta$ that do not cancel and only ratios of $s$ are meaningful. Points are the centres of six equal-width bins. Solid lines: $\log_{10}$ of the mean of $s$ in the bin. Dashed lines: $\log_{10}$ of the median. Colours: $\chi_p$ at $f_{\rm ref}=10$ Hz in $[0,0.4)$ (blue), $[0.4,0.7)$ (orange), $[0.7,1]$ (green). Shaded bands: bootstrap 68 % intervals (1000 resamples). Dotted vertical line: $|\cos\iota_Q(0)|=\sin20^\circ$; to its left is the edge-on region $|\iota_Q(0)-90^\circ|<20^\circ$ used for $R_{\rm edge}$.

Data: 8000 draws from the PE prior of the `C00:NRSur7dq4` label of the GW231123 release, restricted to detector-frame $M\in[287.0,333.2]\,M_\odot$ and $q\in[0.685,0.974]$ (posterior 5–95 %). Sky position fixed at the maximum-likelihood sample; PE PSDs; 20–448 Hz. All 8000 points evaluated with no failures. Derivatives are fourth-order finite differences of the NRSur7dq4 waveform (S1).

What to see: $s$ falls from edge-on to face-on in every $\chi_p$ bin. The medians fall by 1.8 to 2.4 decades across the axis. The edge-on to non-edge-on ratio of medians is $R_{\rm edge}=20.7$, 16.5 and 12.2 for the three $\chi_p$ bins, from low to high, so it does not rise with $\chi_p$. The means lie 1.5 to 5 decades above the medians and jump from bin to bin: in each region fewer than 1 % of the points hold most of the sum (the 10 largest hold 91 % of the edge-on sum). So the mean curves are not converged, and their bands understate the uncertainty.
