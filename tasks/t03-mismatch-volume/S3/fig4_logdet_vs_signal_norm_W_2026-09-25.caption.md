The mechanism behind the edge-on excess of the metric volume: $\sqrt{\det g^\theta}$ grows as the network signal at fixed distance weakens. The plot tests whether the large values of $s$ in figures 1–3 come from orientations in which the H1–L1 network receives a weak signal.

Horizontal axis: $\log_{10}$ of the network $\langle h,h\rangle$ (H1 and L1, 20–448 Hz, PE PSDs), computed with the source at 1000 Mpc and divided by its median over the 8000 points. Vertical axis: $\log_{10}\sqrt{\det g^\theta}$, the metric volume element of the unit-normalised signal over $\theta$ (in the units of $\theta$). Grey dots: prior draws of set W with $|\iota_Q(0)-90^\circ|\ge20^\circ$. Red dots: prior draws with $|\iota_Q(0)-90^\circ|<20^\circ$ (edge-on at merger). Black lines with markers: a controlled test at the three points of largest $s$, all edge-on. At each, only the polarisation angle $\psi$ is set to eight values in $[0,\pi/2)$, all other parameters fixed, and the metric is recomputed. The markers are joined in order of $\langle h,h\rangle$.

Data: the 8000 window prior draws of figure 1. $\langle h,h\rangle$ comes from `S3/signal_norm.py` and the $\psi$ scan from `S3/psi_scan.py`, both with the same waveform and inner product as the metric.

What to see: over W, $\log\sqrt{\det g^\theta}$ falls with $\log\langle h,h\rangle$ (Spearman $-0.65$). Edge-on orientations have lower $\langle h,h\rangle$ (median ratio to non-edge-on 0.29). In the controlled test, changing $\psi$ alone changes $\langle h,h\rangle$ by a factor of 40–80 and $\sqrt{\det g^\theta}$ by 6.5–8.7 decades. The slope is $-3.8$ to $-4.5$ in $\tfrac12\ln\det g^\theta$ per unit $\ln\langle h,h\rangle$, near $-9/2$, the value if every one of the nine metric directions scales as $1/\langle h,h\rangle$. At a weak orientation the unit-normalised waveform is dominated by its small, rapidly varying parts, so it changes fast with the parameters. At three non-edge-on points of median $s$, varying $\psi$ changes $\langle h,h\rangle$ by only 3–23 % (not drawn).
