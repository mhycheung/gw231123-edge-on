Two secondary counts of distinguishable waveforms at the SNR of GW231123, against the inclination at merger, for prior draws in the GW231123 mass window (set W). They show whether the edge-on excess of the metric volume (figure 1) survives when only directions resolved at the event's SNR are counted, and how many extrinsic directions profiling hides.

Upper panel: $\log_{10}N_\rho$, where $N_\rho=\sqrt{\det(I+\rho^2Dg^\theta D)}$, with $\rho=20.6$ the median network matched-filter SNR of the PE and $D$ the diagonal matrix of prior standard deviations of $\theta$ in the window. $N_\rho$ is dimensionless and approximately counts distinguishable waveforms in a prior-sized cell, where a direction contributes only if it is resolved at SNR 20.6. Solid lines: $\log_{10}$ of the bin mean. Dashed lines: $\log_{10}$ of the bin median. Bands: bootstrap 68 % intervals. Lower panel: the mean number of nuisance directions ($\phi_{\rm ref}$ or $\iota$, $\psi$, $t_c$) resolved at SNR 20.6, i.e. eigenvalues of $\rho^2D_\nu CD_\nu>1$, with $C$ the nuisance block of the metric and $D_\nu$ the prior standard deviations of the nuisances; the maximum is 3. Horizontal axis: $|\cos\iota_Q(0)|$ (0 is edge-on), six equal-width bins. Colours: $\chi_p$ bins, as in figure 1. Dotted line: the edge-on boundary.

Data: the same 8000 prior draws as figure 1.

What to see: the median $N_\rho$ falls from about $10^{2.4}$–$10^{2.8}$ (most edge-on bin) to $10^{1.3}$–$10^{1.7}$ (face-on), about one decade, less than the 1.8–2.4 decades of the median of $s$. $N_\rho$ counts only directions resolved at SNR 20.6, so the edge-on excess survives at the event's SNR. All three nuisances are resolved except near face-on, where $\phi_{\rm ref}$ and $\psi$ become degenerate.
