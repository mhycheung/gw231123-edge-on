The density of distinguishable waveforms per unit prior probability, $s$, against the inclination at merger, over the GW231123 posterior (set P). It shows whether the edge-on excess of $s$ seen for prior draws (figure 1) is also present among posterior samples.

Upper panel: as in figure 1. Horizontal axis $|\cos\iota_Q(0)|$ (0 is edge-on); vertical axis $\log_{10}s$, with $s=\sqrt{\det g^\theta}/\pi_\theta$ (only ratios are meaningful). Six equal-width bins. Solid lines: $\log_{10}$ of the bin mean. Dashed lines: $\log_{10}$ of the bin median. Shaded bands: bootstrap 68 % intervals (1000 resamples). Orange: $0.4\le\chi_p<0.7$; green: $0.7\le\chi_p\le1$. The $\chi_p<0.4$ bin holds one posterior point and is not drawn. Bins with fewer than 10 points are omitted. Dotted line: the edge-on boundary $|\iota_Q(0)-90^\circ|=20^\circ$. Lower panel: the number of posterior points per bin of $|\cos\iota_Q(0)|$ (24 bins of width 1/24).

Data: 2001 samples of the `C00:NRSur7dq4` posterior (2000 random with seed 1, plus the maximum-likelihood sample), with $\iota_Q(0)$ computed with NRSur7dq4 (v1). All evaluated with no failures. The mean over P is a posterior average of $s$, not a metric volume per unit prior probability.

What to see: 81 % of the posterior points are edge-on. Among posterior samples, the median $s$ barely depends on $\iota_Q(0)$: the ratio of edge-on to non-edge-on medians is 1.13 (68 %: 1.03–1.21). The large ratio between the edge-on and face-on medians seen for prior draws (figure 1) is absent. The mean in the most edge-on bin is dominated by a few points, as in figure 1.
