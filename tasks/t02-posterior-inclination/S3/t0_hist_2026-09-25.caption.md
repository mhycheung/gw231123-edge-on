Distributions of the orbital inclination of GW231123 at merger ($t = 0$), for the `C00:NRSur7dq4` posterior and its prior, compared with the inclination at the reference frequency; it answers how much closer to edge-on ($90^\circ$) the posterior is at merger than at $f_{\rm ref} = 10$ Hz, and whether the prior shows the same shift.

The left panel is the distribution of $\iota_Q(t = 0)$, the angle between the line of sight $\hat N$ and the axis of the surrogate's coprecessing frame (the frame that follows the precession of the orbital plane) at $t = 0$. The right panel is the distribution of $\iota_E(t = 0)$, the angle between $\hat N$ and the direction in which the strain amplitude $|h(t=0,\hat n)|$ is largest, on the side where the phase of $h$ decreases in time. The horizontal axes are these angles in degrees from $0^\circ$ to $180^\circ$; the vertical axis, the same for both panels, is the normalised density in $\mathrm{deg}^{-1}$, with 60 bins of $3^\circ$. The blue filled histogram is the posterior and the orange filled histogram is the prior. The black histograms, identical in both panels, are the inclination $\iota$ at $f_{\rm ref}$ as sampled by the parameter estimation: solid for the posterior, dotted for the prior. The dashed vertical line marks $90^\circ$ (edge-on). Here $t = 0$ is the surrogate's time origin, the peak of the total amplitude summed over modes.

The data are all 18185 posterior samples and all 5000 stored prior samples of the `C00:NRSur7dq4` label of the GW231123 release (Zenodo record 17437902), each evaluated with the NRSur7dq4v2 surrogate at $f_{\rm ref} = 10$ Hz with no failures; the parameter estimation itself used NRSur7dq4 (v1). $\iota_E(0)$ comes from a full search over the sphere of directions at $t = 0$. The modes with odd $m$ tilt the direction of maximum emission away from the orbital axis by several degrees, so $\iota_E$ and $\iota_Q$ are related but not equal. The prior is isotropic in the spin directions and in the orientation of the total angular momentum.

What to see: the posterior $\iota$ at $f_{\rm ref}$ is bimodal, with modes near $50^\circ$ and $130^\circ$ and almost no weight near $90^\circ$ (median $59.2^\circ$, 90 % interval [$35.3^\circ$, $137.5^\circ$]). At $t = 0$ both modes have moved toward edge-on, to near $78^\circ$ and $101^\circ$: $\iota_Q(0) = 82.3^\circ$ [$60.7^\circ$, $109.3^\circ$] and $\iota_E(0) = 82.7^\circ$ [$59.3^\circ$, $110.4^\circ$]. The fraction of posterior samples with $|\iota - 90^\circ| < 20^\circ$ rises from 0.027 at $f_{\rm ref}$ to 0.823 for $\iota_Q(0)$ and 0.799 for $\iota_E(0)$; within $10^\circ$ it rises from 0.001 to 0.332 and 0.283. The prior distributions at $t = 0$ match the prior at $f_{\rm ref}$ (fraction within $20^\circ$: 0.353 at $f_{\rm ref}$, 0.332 for $\iota_Q(0)$, 0.342 for $\iota_E(0)$), so the shift toward edge-on is a property of the posterior, not of the calculation.
