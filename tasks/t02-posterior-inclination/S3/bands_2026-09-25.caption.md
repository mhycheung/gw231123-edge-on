Pointwise credible bands of the orbital inclination of GW231123 against time, over the whole `C00:NRSur7dq4` posterior and over draws from its prior; it answers whether the data favour an orientation that becomes edge-on ($\iota = 90^\circ$) at merger, beyond what precession alone would produce for any parameter draw.

Both panels show inclination $\iota$ in degrees ($0^\circ$ to $180^\circ$) against time $t/M$, where $M$ is the detector-frame total mass of each sample and $t = 0$ is the surrogate's time origin, the peak of the total amplitude summed over modes. The left panel covers $-4300M$ to $+100M$; the right panel shows $-250M$ to $+50M$ on an expanded scale. Blue is the posterior and orange is the prior; where they overlap the shading appears grey-brown. Solid lines and bands are $\iota_Q(t)$, the angle between the line of sight $\hat N$ and the axis of the surrogate's coprecessing frame (the frame that follows the precession of the orbital plane). Dashed lines and bands are $\iota_E(t)$, the angle between $\hat N$ and the direction in which the strain amplitude $|h(t,\hat n)|$ is largest, taken on the side where the phase of $h$ decreases in time. For each set, the lighter shading is the 5 to 95 % range (90 %), the darker shading is the 16 to 84 % range (68 %), and the line is the median. The black dotted horizontal line marks $90^\circ$. The grey vertical line and grey band mark the median and 5 to 95 % range of the posterior reference time $t_{\rm ref}$, where the orbit reaches $f_{\rm ref} = 10$ Hz: $-217.7M$ [$-239.5M$, $-191.1M$]. The black bar at the left edge of the left panel is the posterior $\iota$ at $f_{\rm ref}$ as sampled by the parameter estimation (lighter: 90 %, darker: 68 %, line: median $59.2^\circ$).

The data are all 18185 posterior samples and all 5000 stored prior samples of the `C00:NRSur7dq4` label of the GW231123 release (Zenodo record 17437902), each evaluated with the NRSur7dq4v2 surrogate at $f_{\rm ref} = 10$ Hz, with no failures. The parameter estimation itself used NRSur7dq4 (v1) through LALSimulation. $\iota_Q$ is computed on a $1M$ grid from $-4300M$ to $+100M$ (the first point is undefined in every sample and is not drawn). $\iota_E$ is found by a full-sphere search at $t = 0$ and then followed backward and forward in time, on 91 times from $-1000M$ to $+50M$, so the dashed bands start at $-1000M$. The bands are equal-weight percentiles over samples at each time. The prior is isotropic in the spin directions and in the orientation of the total angular momentum. Two features limit the reading. First, the posterior $\iota$ is bimodal about $90^\circ$ (modes near $50^\circ$ and $130^\circ$ at $f_{\rm ref}$, near $78^\circ$ and $101^\circ$ at $t = 0$), so the posterior median line lies between the modes and does not describe a typical sample; the folded plot of $|\iota - 90^\circ|$ avoids this. Second, $\iota_Q$ is constant for $t \gtrsim +20M$ in every sample; the project infers, without a test, that the coprecessing frame stops evolving after merger, so the bands after merger should not be interpreted. In 11.3 % of prior samples, and in no posterior sample, the followed $\iota_E$ jumps to the other emission maximum at some time, so part of the prior $\iota_E$ band follows the opposite lobe.

What to see: the prior bands are flat in time (median $|\iota_Q - 90^\circ|$ is $29.4^\circ$ at $f_{\rm ref}$ and $29.9^\circ$ at $t = 0$), which shows that the calculation by itself does not move the inclination toward edge-on. The posterior bands narrow toward $90^\circ$ between $t_{\rm ref}$ and merger: $\iota_Q(0) = 82.3^\circ$ [$60.7^\circ$, $109.3^\circ$] and $\iota_E(0) = 82.7^\circ$ [$59.3^\circ$, $110.4^\circ$] (median and 90 % interval), against $\iota = 59.2^\circ$ [$35.3^\circ$, $137.5^\circ$] at $f_{\rm ref}$. $\iota_Q$ and $\iota_E$ agree closely before merger.
