Pointwise bands of the angle from edge-on, $|\iota - 90^\circ|$, against time over the GW231123 `C00:NRSur7dq4` posterior and its prior; it answers how close to edge-on the binary is at each time, in a form that is not distorted by the bimodality of $\iota$ about $90^\circ$.

Both panels show $|\iota - 90^\circ|$ in degrees ($0^\circ$ = edge-on, $90^\circ$ = face-on or face-off) against time $t/M$, where $M$ is the detector-frame total mass of each sample and $t = 0$ is the surrogate's time origin, the peak of the total amplitude summed over modes. The left panel covers $-4300M$ to $+100M$; the right panel shows $-250M$ to $+50M$ on an expanded scale. Blue is the posterior and orange is the prior. Solid lines are $\iota_Q(t)$, the angle between the line of sight $\hat N$ and the axis of the surrogate's coprecessing frame (the frame that follows the precession of the orbital plane); dashed lines are $\iota_E(t)$, the angle between $\hat N$ and the direction in which the strain amplitude $|h(t,\hat n)|$ is largest, on the side where the phase of $h$ decreases in time. Thick lines are pointwise medians and thin lines are pointwise 95th percentiles. The shading is the 16 to 84 % range (68 %) of $\iota_Q$ only; it appears grey-brown where the blue and orange bands overlap. The grey vertical line and grey band mark the median and 5 to 95 % range of the posterior reference time $t_{\rm ref}$, where the orbit reaches $f_{\rm ref} = 10$ Hz: $-217.7M$ [$-239.5M$, $-191.1M$]. The black bar at the left edge of the left panel is the posterior $|\iota - 90^\circ|$ at $f_{\rm ref}$ (shading: 68 %, line: median $39.0^\circ$).

The data are all 18185 posterior and all 5000 stored prior samples of the `C00:NRSur7dq4` label (Zenodo record 17437902), each evaluated with the NRSur7dq4v2 surrogate at $f_{\rm ref} = 10$ Hz with no failures; the parameter estimation itself used NRSur7dq4 (v1). $\iota_Q$ is on a $1M$ grid from $-4300M$ to $+100M$; $\iota_E$ is found by a full-sphere search at $t = 0$ and followed in time on 91 times from $-1000M$ to $+50M$, so the dashed lines start at $-1000M$. Percentiles are equal-weight over samples at each time. This figure was added outside the original plan because $\iota$ itself is bimodal about $90^\circ$ in the posterior, which makes the median of $\iota$ misleading; $|\iota - 90^\circ|$ is unimodal. $\iota_Q$ is constant for $t \gtrsim +20M$ in every sample, which the project infers, without a test, to mean that the coprecessing frame stops evolving after merger; the curves after merger should not be interpreted. In 11.3 % of prior samples the followed $\iota_E$ jumps to the other emission maximum at some time.

What to see: under the prior the distance from edge-on does not change with time (median $29.4^\circ$ at $f_{\rm ref}$, $29.9^\circ$ at $t = 0$). Under the posterior the median $|\iota_Q - 90^\circ|$ falls from $39.0^\circ$ at $f_{\rm ref}$ to $12.6^\circ$ at $t = 0$, with a minimum of $8.2^\circ$ at $t = -12M$, well below the prior. The posterior probability that $|\iota_Q(0) - 90^\circ| < 20^\circ$ is 0.82, against 0.33 for the prior and 0.027 for the posterior $\iota$ at $f_{\rm ref}$.
